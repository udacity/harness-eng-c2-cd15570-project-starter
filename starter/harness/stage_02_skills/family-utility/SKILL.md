# Family & Utility Evaluation

Use this skill when practicality matters.

## Passenger capacity

Seat count is a hard constraint only when the buyer regularly carries that many people.
A five-seat vehicle may be adequate for a family of five, but comfort and cargo can still matter.

## Cargo

Cargo volume matters for:
- Strollers
- Sports equipment
- Luggage
- Grocery hauling
- Pets
- Camping equipment

Do not infer cargo capacity from body style alone. Verify the actual cargo_cuft field.

## AWD

AWD can help with:
- Low-traction conditions
- Snow/rain confidence
- Some unpaved access

AWD does not automatically mean serious off-road capability.

## Road trips

For frequent long trips, consider:
- Seating capacity
- cargo
- range/refueling/charging convenience
- highway efficiency
- comfort
- reliability

## Towing and outdoor use

A tow package or adventure-oriented vehicle may matter for trailers or outdoor equipment. Do not assume all SUVs tow equally.

## Recommendation method

1. Apply hard capacity constraints first.
2. Compare multiple surviving categories.
3. Balance purchase cost with operating cost and reliability.
4. Use owner feedback to catch practical complaints that specifications miss.
